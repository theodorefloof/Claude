## 1. Design stance

This skeleton keeps the decision-first spine (the highest-scoring design across the three judges) and fixes what all three judges faulted. It optimises for five shared, specific, defensible answers taken in the right order: the four hardest upstream calls (hero mechanic, single-player value, who pays first, money shown or hidden) at 09:10 on Saturday from sealed written positions, founder commitment and decision rights in their own 15-minute slot immediately after, Q5 and Q2 before lunch, scope locked and signed at 12:10, Q3 on Saturday at 17:20 after a transition, and Q4 first thing on Sunday with fresh heads. Implementation is protected the build-first way: 4 hours 05 minutes of Saturday build with the minimum demo (CP3) at 16:00, the six acceptance tests run on Saturday at 17:00 so a fix list exists before dinner, a Sunday build that does fixes first and touches the map only if the fix list is empty, polish gated behind an empty fix list, and a 30-minute reserve at the end of each day that the build draws first and that is never spent on new features. Evidence discipline is taken from the evidence-first design: six labels defined on page one, a Validated finding needs a count, a date and a founder's name and fewer than five observations stays a hypothesis, "not found publicly" wording for anything about an alternative, a ten-minute cheat test on the prototype, and the rule that nothing becomes validated because the demo worked or because both founders agree. What is deliberately sacrificed: prototype polish (wallet card, map, add-product sheet are the first cuts and appear on Sunday only if nothing is broken); the full seven-hour build plan in prototype_feasibility.md; the Wizard of Oz week and community intercepts as required preparation (both stay optional and become the first behavioural tests after the workshop); a long deck-writing session (Tory drafts the narrative and headlines on Saturday, Q4 decides on Sunday, the slide table is filled during the build); and a leisurely Sunday (lunch and the afternoon break are real but sit back to back, and the final review is 40 minutes with a 30-minute reserve behind it). Hands-on build time is 5 hours 35 minutes in the primary schedule and 6 hours 40 minutes in the extension, and every minute of both days is accounted for.

## 12. Decision rule for disagreements and how uncertain decisions are recorded

The rules card, signed at D1-01:

1. Positions are written before they are spoken. Every shared session opens with a written-alone step; each founder writes their position, the strongest argument for the other side, and what evidence would change their mind. Nothing written is edited after the swap. The divergence list is on the table so discussion covers listed disagreements only.
2. The timebox is the arbiter. The session lead ends each step at its mark and calls the decision when the writing step starts. A logged decision is not reopened on the same day unless a new fact appears (not a new argument); reopen requests go to the parking lot and are triaged at D1-10, D2-01 and D2-06.
3. Domain owner decides provisionally after hearing the other. Product definition, UX, journey, prototype scope, product priorities and the do-not-build list: Blake. Positioning, ICP, buyer, offer, pricing hypotheses, first customers and the deck's argument: Tory. Q2 (what it does) is Blake's domain; Q3 (how we sell it) is Tory's domain. Q1 (what Sway is), Q5 (dramatically better than Duel), Q4 (how we raise capital), the launch city, founder commitment and decision rights are joint: both founders must agree, and an unresolved joint item goes to rule 4, never to a domain owner. Tory's view as originator of the idea is recorded verbatim on Q1 and Q5 whenever the founders differ; Blake abstains on any non-public statement about Duel and the abstention is logged each time. The deciding founder must first restate the other's objection in one sentence to the other's satisfaction.
4. Joint items unresolved at the timebox go to "reversible and testable wins": choose the option that is cheaper to reverse and produces observable evidence sooner. If still tied, the option closer to the current source-of-truth documents stands (older scope is never restored silently), and the decision is labelled Provisional with the dissent verbatim.
5. Every log entry carries exactly one of six labels: Decision (both agree; stands until a review trigger fires); Provisional (the decision rule applied; dissent recorded verbatim); Draft (text that exists but is not yet agreed); Demonstration (something the prototype shows; never evidence of demand); Validated finding (an observation from real people, with a count, a date and the reporting founder's name; fewer than five observations stays a Hypothesis in the documents and an Open question in the log); Open question (no decision; owner and trigger assigned). Nothing becomes a Validated finding because the demo worked, because both founders agree, or because a research file inferred it. The deck, the sales strategy and the product definition stay Draft wherever their claims rest on a Hypothesis, and say so in the document.
6. A Provisional decision or Open question is not complete without a review trigger in three parts: the observation that would reopen it (a metric or an event, in numbers where possible), the date by which it is checked, and the founder who reports. Worked example: "Hero mechanic = Sway Pass. Provisional. Dissent: Tory expects the card in the chat to be used more. Trigger: reopen if the Pass is offered in fewer than one in five recommendation moments in the six post-workshop cold tests and the first cohort's first two weeks, or if median scan-to-page exceeds 15 seconds; check Sun 18 Oct 2026 and Fri 20 Nov 2026; owner Blake." Other trigger forms the workshop should use: "reopen if fewer than 3 of 10 cold-test recipients tap the dominant action first, by Fri 20 Nov, owner Blake"; "reopen if the first brand marketer to hear the offer names a different budget line, by Fri 23 Oct, owner Tory"; "reopen if fewer than 40 per cent of products in the first 50 Lockers are wellness-brand products, by Fri 11 Dec, owner Blake".
7. The prototype wins in the room, the evidence wins afterwards. If a sales or deck claim needs something the prototype does not show, the claim is downgraded to Hypothesis, never the other way round; the scope lock is never reopened to rescue a claim. Every claim about an alternative cites a URL or a founder's own test note and says "not found publicly" rather than "does not have"; nothing about Duel that is not on a public page enters any document.
8. No decisions by exhaustion. No strategic decision is made after 18:00 on Saturday or inside a reserve; anything still open goes to the log as Provisional with a date. Timekeeping is not a disagreement: the reserve is spent only by the lead, and the Sunday hard stop is not negotiable.
9. Tie-breakers during the build: Tory on the cut list (she strikes, Blake cannot add); Blake on whether a fix is attempted or moved to known limitations. Neither reopens scope after 12:10 Saturday.

Recording: one row per decision in the decision log with the fixed columns (section 6). Evidence labels in every document follow the research files: [DECIDED], [HYPOTHESIS], [ILLUSTRATIVE], [NEEDS SOURCE], [UNVERIFIED], [SNIPPET], [FETCHED], [VENDOR-PUBLISHED], [FOUNDER ASSERTION], plus Validated finding with count, date and founder's name. Labels are policed at D2-06; a Provisional decision without a review trigger is not allowed to leave the room.
