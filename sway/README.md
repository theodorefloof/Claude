# Sway Barcelona programme

The preparation plan, the two workshop days in Barcelona on Sat 10 and Sun 11 October 2026, and the week that follows, for Blake (product lead) and Tori (sales lead).

The shared, editable version is the website: https://claude.ai/artifact/EBW6KMHCkfar4HuDBhtFJZ

- `site/` is that website: one page that renders every document, with shared inputs both founders can edit from different countries.
- `barcelona-programme.md` is the whole programme in one file, about 72,000 words.
- `sections/` holds the same content split into the eleven sections, in reading order.
- `00_BRIEF.md` restates the founders' brief and constraints.
- `research/` holds the public-source research, the context audit, the investor lens and three skeptic reviews used as inputs. Evidence labels are inside each file.
- `design/` holds the three candidate skeletons, the final skeleton, the resolution plan that reconciled the audit findings, and the change spec for the compressed preparation plan.

Preparation is four evenings, Mon 5 to Thu 8 October, plus three admin anchors due Fri 25 September. All Duel material comes from public sources only. Flights, accommodation and availability are unconfirmed, so the calendar is provisional where labelled.
