# Sway Barcelona programme

The complete preparation and workshop programme for the Sway founders' two working days in Barcelona on Sat 10 and Sun 11 October 2026, plus the research it is built on.

- `barcelona-programme.md` is the whole programme in one file (about 72,000 words).
- `sections/` holds the same content split into the eleven sections, in reading order.
- `00_BRIEF.md` restates the founders' brief and constraints.
- `research/` holds the public-source research, the context audit, the investor lens and three skeptic reviews used as inputs. Evidence labels are inside each file.
- `design/` holds the three candidate programme skeletons, the synthesised final skeleton, and the resolution plan that reconciled the audit findings.

All Duel material comes from public sources only; no internal material was used. Flights, accommodation and daily availability are unconfirmed, so the calendar is provisional where labelled.
