# Duel (duel.tech): what it demonstrably offers today, from public sources only

Prepared: Wednesday 16 September 2026, for the Sway founders' workshop (Barcelona, Sat 10 and Sun 11 October 2026).
Scope: public information only. No internal Duel material was used. Facts only; no assessment of strengths or weaknesses in this file.

## How to read the evidence labels

Every fact below carries one of these labels:

- **Search snippet**: text returned by a web search against the named URL. The primary page itself could not be fetched because the research environment's network gateway blocked every non-search domain tried (full list in section 7). Treat snippets as indicative of page content on the crawl date, not as a verified copy.
- **Press release (via snippet)**: the 23 September 2025 BusinessWire release, seen only through search snippets from BusinessWire, Yahoo Finance, Morningstar, Bright Pixel and other syndications. Wording appeared consistently across several syndications, so confidence in the quoted wording is higher than for a single snippet.
- **Third-party estimate**: a data vendor's figure (headcount, revenue) with no Duel confirmation. Always marked [UNVERIFIED].
- **[NEEDS SOURCE]**: a claim that appeared in a search summary without a clear originating URL, or that the founders should confirm directly.

Style note: dashes inside quoted source text have been replaced with commas or colons to comply with the programme's house style. Wording is otherwise unchanged.
