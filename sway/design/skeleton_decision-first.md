# Sway programme skeleton: decision-first design

Prepared Wednesday 16 September 2026. Workshop Saturday 10 and Sunday 11 October 2026, Barcelona (CEST, UTC+2). All clock times below are Barcelona local time unless marked UK.

Labels: [ASSUMPTION] marks a provisional planning assumption the founders must confirm. SUGGESTED marks a preparation slot that is proposed, not booked. Journey roles are written as "the advocate" and "the recipient"; the package's persona names (Tori, Blake) are not used, and the prototype's on-screen persona names are chosen at D1-04 (placeholders: Maya recommends, Sam scans).

## 1. Design stance

This skeleton optimises for the quality and defensibility of the five answers and the handful of decisions that everything else depends on. The five hardest calls (hero mechanic, single-player value, who pays first, money on or off, founder commitment) are taken at 09:10 on Saturday, before any building, using written-alone-then-compare in every shared session, with the written part done in preparation so the room spends its minutes comparing rather than composing. The prototype gets 5 hours 35 minutes of hands-on time across the two days (5 minutes above the floor), sequenced so the minimum demo (CP3) exists by 16:10 on Saturday and Sunday's short morning is fixes and run-throughs only. Tory's commercial track runs in parallel with the build and is reconciled in three short compare sessions rather than long shared drafting. What is deliberately sacrificed: prototype polish (the map, the wallet mock and the add-product sheet are the first cuts), the full seven-hour build plan in prototype_feasibility.md, a non-working Sunday lunch, in-room drafting time for Q3 and Q4 (both are drafted by Tory alone and confirmed in 25 and 30 minute sessions), and any optional preparation beyond about 10 hours 30 minutes per founder. Every day carries a 30-minute reserve at its end that absorbs overruns; unused reserve ends the day early and is never spent on new features.

### D1-02 Q1 What is Sway: the five hardest calls (70 min, 09:10 to 10:20)

- Purpose: answer Q1 (category, target customer, founding insight, positioning, reason to exist) and settle the five calls everything else depends on: (a) hero mechanic: Sway Pass, Sway Link, or a card sent into the chat, with the Pass second; (b) the single-player value sentence, what one person gets on day one with nobody else on it; (c) who pays first and when brands enter: consumer-first alone, consumer-first with two named design-partner brands as unpaid observers, or a paid discovery study as the first offer; (d) money shown or hidden in the first cohort (earnings label on or off by default); (e) founder commitment: hours per week now, the trigger and date for one founder going full time, and who owns product, pilot recruitment, commercial outreach, operations and fundraising.
- Inputs: each founder's own Founder answers v2; context_audit.md sections 4 and 5 (assumptions 1 to 3 and 6; tensions 5.1, 5.2, 5.4, 5.14); challenge_consumer-behaviour.md prompts 2, 5, 11 and 14; challenge_investor-skeptic.md section 6; challenge_brand-buyer.md prompt 10.
- Method: (10) Written alone: each re-reads their v2 and writes the Q1 card: one-sentence description; the customer problem; the five calls in one line each with reasoning and "what evidence would change my mind". (10) Swap and read silently; mark each line agree / differ / unsure. (30) Compare, hardest first, six minutes per call: two minutes each to state the position, two minutes of discussion, then decide or apply the decision rule and record as Provisional with a review trigger. (10) Write the Q1 answer block together on the thesis one-pager: category, target customer, founding insight, positioning line, reason to exist. (10) Log all five calls with labels and dissent verbatim; call (e) is recorded as names, hours and dates, and marked Provisional if it depends on advice not yet received.
- Lead: Blake. Recorder: Tory. Artifact owner: Tory (one-page company thesis).
- Output: Q1 answer block under 120 words; five logged calls.
- Completion criterion: Q1 answer is a form both would say to a stranger; each call has a label, and every Provisional call has a named observation, date and owner as its trigger.
- Fallback: a call unresolved at its six-minute mark goes to the decision rule immediately; if two calls remain open at 09:50, they are logged as Open questions with triggers and Q1 is written without them; the day does not extend beyond the reserve.

## 12. Decision rule for disagreements and how uncertain decisions are recorded

1. Positions are written before they are spoken. In every shared session each founder writes their position, the strongest argument for the other side, and what evidence would change their mind, before comparison starts. Nothing written is edited after the swap.
2. Domain owner decides after hearing the other, within the timebox. Product, UX, journey, prototype scope: Blake. Positioning, buyer, offer, pricing hypotheses, first customers: Tory. The five Q1 calls, the one-sentence description, the launch city, the fundraising narrative, founder commitment and decision rights are joint and cannot be settled by domain.
3. Joint items unresolved at the timebox go to "reversible and testable wins": choose the option that is cheaper to reverse and produces observable evidence sooner. If still tied, the option closer to the current source-of-truth documents stands (older scope is never restored silently), and the decision is labelled Provisional.
4. Every log entry carries exactly one label: Decision (both agree, stands until a review trigger fires), Provisional (decision rule applied, dissent recorded verbatim), Draft (text that exists but is not yet agreed), Demonstration (something the prototype shows; never evidence of demand), Validated finding (an observation from a real person, dated, with the source), Open question (no decision; owner and trigger assigned).
5. A Provisional decision or Open question is not complete without a review trigger in three parts: the observation that would reopen it (a metric or an event, in numbers where possible), the date by which it is checked (the first cohort read or a calendar date), and the founder who reports. Example: "Hero mechanic = Sway Pass. Provisional. Dissent: Tory expects the card in the chat to be used more. Trigger: if the Pass is offered in fewer than one in five recommendation moments in the two-week tally of the first five members, reopen; check by Fri 20 Nov 2026; owner Blake."
6. A logged decision is not reopened on the same day unless a new fact appears (not a new argument). Reopen requests go to the parking lot and are triaged at D1-09, D2-01 and D2-06.
7. Timekeeping is not a disagreement. The session lead ends the timebox; the reserve is spent only by the lead; the Sunday hard stop is not negotiable.
8. Tory is the tie-breaker on the cut list during the build; Blake is the tie-breaker on whether a fix is attempted or moved to known limitations. Neither reopens scope after 12:25 Saturday.
