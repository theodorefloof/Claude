Across the whole of production
The system works across the whole of production,
not only the making.
Work runs through three stages, and specific tasks are switched on at each one. This is what answers what happens before production, during it, and after it.
Before production
Reading the brief and working out what is actually needed
During production
Making what does not already exist, and assembling the rest
After production
Turning one approved output into all the versions that are needed
Search your DAM for what exists
Break the brief into components
Check brand, claims and rights rules
Produce the components that are new
Assemble them into each output
Hold every version in one place
Versioning across formats
Translation and localisation
Delivery to your channels
your review
your approval
People are in the loop at the points that matter.   Your team reviews before production begins and approves before anything is released. Everything between those two points is ours to run.
