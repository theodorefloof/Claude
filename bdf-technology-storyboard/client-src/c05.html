Modularity
A brief only uses the parts of the system it needs.
MAKE is a set of modules, not one product you switch on whole. A single social cut-down touches two of them. A nine-market campaign touches all of them. There is nothing to implement twice, and nothing for your team to learn about the parts a brief never uses.
