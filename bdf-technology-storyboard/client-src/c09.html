What it means in practice
Your second market orders what already exists.
It does not brief again.
Because every brief leaves its components and a record behind, the next market can search, find and order. Language, format and local claims become choices on a form rather than a new brief, a new schedule and a new cost.
Instead of this
A second brief, a second concept, a second shoot, a second review.
The whole chain on page 07 run again, at close to full cost and full timeline, to produce something that is a near neighbour of an asset already sitting in your DAM.
