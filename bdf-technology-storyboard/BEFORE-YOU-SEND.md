# Client deck: what to complete before it goes out

Companion to `dist/technology-client.*` (11 pages). This is the submittable version. Every internal
flag has been removed, so nothing in it says "to be confirmed" to the client. The three items below
marked **FIELD** are visible blanks for you to fill. The items marked **DECIDE** are positions the
deck now takes that need your sign-off, because I took them on your behalf.

---

## 1. Three fields to fill in

| Page | What is blank | What to put there |

box in a client submission.

## 2. Four positions I took that need your sign-off

**DECIDE · Page 09, the back catalogue.** This is the one to read carefully. The client deck now
states a position: material already in the DAM gets described at the point it is first reused, so
there is no cataloguing project to complete before the benefit starts, and we prioritise part of the
library ahead of demand only if it is worth it. That is the approach that needs no technical proof
and adds no cost line. It is deliberately **not** the automated-enrichment story (nobody could
confirm it is possible) and **not** a paid curation resource (that would need costing first). If you
want either of those instead, this page has to change and something has to be costed.

**DECIDE · Page 03, the route descriptions.** I wrote the three routes from how you described them
in the call: assisted is our team leading with the system assisting, augmented is the system carrying
most of the sequence with our team directing, automated is the system running it end to end. Check
that against the definitions in the brief before this goes out. The balance bars are labelled as
indicative rather than measured, on purpose.

**DECIDE · Page 02, the worked example.** The seasonal launch film and its nine components are mine,
labelled as an example. It is the clearest way to explain modularity, but a real cleared example from
our own work would be stronger. The arithmetic on the page (nine components, six outputs) follows the
example, so it changes if the example changes.

**DECIDE · Page 07, portability.** The page shows the shape of the answer: your DAM stays the source
of truth, everything registers back, nothing is held back. It does not contain a formal portability
commitment (what you keep, in what format, what happens at exit). If the submission needs that
stated, it belongs here or in the commercial section.

## 3. What is deliberately absent

- **No percentage anywhere, including the 80% figure.** It was never defined, so putting it in front
  of a client would be the most quotable and least defensible thing in the section.
- **No tool names.** The split between the creation tool and the aggregating tool is unconfirmed, and
  what was shown in preparation was not the tool intended for the pitch. Naming tools in writing
  risks contradicting the live session. Page 04 describes them by category instead.
- **No dates.** Page 10 says so explicitly, and frames timings as belonging in the plan.
- **"Maker Studio" does not appear.** Nor does "makers". The product is MAKE throughout.

---

## 4. Design steps

The deck uses black, greys and one accent (`#1D5C8C`), applied consistently: accent means either
"yours" or the one thing on the page to look at. Swapping that single value re-skins the whole deck.
Beyond that: apply house fonts, add a cover and the client name if the submission needs one, and
renumber the pages if this section sits inside a larger document.
