THE POINT OF THIS SLIDE
The middleware is the answer to “it needs building”. The orchestration layer is built. Tools plug into it and can be swapped without rebuilding anything. Nothing in this proposal asks you to replace a tool you already run, and nothing waits on a platform being finished.
OPENTool names are deliberately absent. The split between the creation tool and the aggregating tool for the live demonstration has not been confirmed, and what was shown in preparation was not the tool intended for the pitch. Until the demo lead confirms, naming tools here risks contradicting the live session. Needed before: the live rehearsal.
