chosen per deliverable at triage.
They are not three stages of production, and they are not a human versus machine split. What changes between them is how much of the sequence AI tasks carry. What never changes is the approval gate.

WHY THIS REPLACES THE EARLIER VISUAL
The earlier “human plus tech / fully automated” diagram described the production step only. It left out briefing and triage, so it read as a split between people and machines rather than three routes. Routes run brief to delivery. Versioning and translation sit inside the automated route, not outside the model.
