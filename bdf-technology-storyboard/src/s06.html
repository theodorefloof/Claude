04  ·  Modularity
A brief uses the modules it needs.
Not every brief uses every module.
MAKE is a set of modules, not one product you switch on whole. The same platform serves a single-asset request and a nine-market campaign, with nothing to re-implement in between.

WHY THIS ANSWERS “TOO COMPLEX”
Complexity is only a problem if you have to absorb all of it. Brief A touches two modules. Nobody on your side configures, licences or learns the other six. The system scales up to Brief B without a second implementation, and back down again without a decommission.
