THE LINE WE DO NOT CROSS
MAKE never becomes your system of record. It is where work is produced, not where it lives. Your DAM is complete at every point in this loop, and it is more complete after each brief than before it.
OPENPortability is one of the three things this section has to answer, alongside integration and data. This loop is the shape of the answer, but the actual commitment is not written: what you keep, in what formats, and what happens to the production record if the relationship ends.
