THE POINT
The order is a form, not a brief. What can be ordered is exactly what the production record says already exists. Nobody writes a creative brief, nobody schedules a shoot, and nobody has to know the first market’s production history to place the order.
DEPENDENCYThis works from day one for everything produced in the system. It does not work, on its own, for the assets already sitting in your DAM from before the engagement, because those have no production record and no component breakdown. That gap is the next slide, and it is the single largest open item in this section.
